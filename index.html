How to play
  • Each base shows its troops. Your bases (blue) and enemy bases grow over time; grey neutral ones don't.
  • Drag from one of your bases to any other region to send a stream of dots there.
  • Sweep over several of your bases while dragging to attack with all of them at once.
  • Or click your base(s) to select, then click a target. Shift-click adds to the selection, A selects all.
  • Each dot that lands removes one defender. Bring more than they have to capture the region.
  • Enemy dots that meet yours in the field destroy each other one for one.
  • Send to your own region to reinforce it. Keys 1–4 choose 25/50/75/100% of troops.
  • Bigger regions grow faster and hold more. Take every enemy base to win.
  • Some neutral bases carry a badge. Hold one and all your bases get a bonus, which stacks: Biology lab Engineering works Construction yard They're better defended, and easier levels have more of them.
